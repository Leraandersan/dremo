She works the late shift at the rooftop bar where billionaire Adrian Cole drinks alone every Friday — and pretends not to notice her. Lila Reyes has rent to pay, two student loans, and a strict rule about never mixing work with anything else. But one reckless night, one misread glance, and one impossible offer change everything. By 3 a.m., they have a deal that neither of them can take back. By morning, the whole city wants to know who Mrs. Cole really is — including Adrian's ex-fiancée, his board of directors, and the man Lila thought she was over.
