After the worst night of her life, struggling barista Lila Reyes wakes up in a Vegas penthouse with a hangover, a wedding ring she doesn't remember saying yes to, and a husband who happens to be Adrian Cole — the ruthless billionaire CEO who's set to fire her at 9 a.m. sharp. Now, with a contract that says they have 30 days to keep up the act or lose everything, Lila must survive Adrian's icy world of boardrooms, jealous ex-fiancées, and feelings she swore she'd never have. But Adrian has a secret of his own — and the longer they pretend, the harder it gets to remember what was a mistake to begin with.
