🏠 House Rules

RULE 01

Be kind. Always.

Disagreement is welcome. Cruelty is not. We moderate quickly because fandom should feel fun, safe, and welcoming — not like a battlefield.

RULE 02

Tag your spoilers.

New viewers join every minute. Use the spoiler tag (⚠️) so everyone gets to experience the gasps for the first time. Theory rooms are exempt — they're spoiler-friendly by default.

RULE 03

No harassment. No ragebait. No pile-ons.

This isn't a marketplace and it isn't a stage. Self-promo, brigading, and bad-faith arguments get zero airtime. The drama belongs on screen.
