Frequently asked questions

Can I really cancel anytime?

Yes. One click. No "are you sure?" rabbit holes. You keep access until the end of your paid period.

What are Alternative Endings?

For select originals, the community votes on the ending. Members on 4-Week and Annual plans get to vote.

How does the Watch Together Bundle work?

You and 1–3 friends each purchase. Once everyone's in, the discount applies retroactively and you get a private group room.

Are there really no coin traps?

None. No paywalls mid-episode. No "unlock for 12 coins." If you're on a plan, every episode is unlocked.

Do I need an account to watch free episodes?

No. First 3 episodes of every series are open to anyone. To comment or join a fandom room, you'll need a free account.

What happens after the 1-Week Pass ends?

It doesn't auto-renew. You can renew, upgrade, or just keep watching free episodes. No surprise charges.
