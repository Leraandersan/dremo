Our House Rules

We take care of this space because it takes care of us
RULE 01

Be kind. Always.

Disagreement is welcome. Cruelty is not. We moderate quickly because women deserve a space online that doesn't feel like a battlefield.

RULE 02

Tag your spoilers.

New viewers join every minute. Use the spoiler tag (⚠️) so everyone gets to experience the gasps for the first time.

RULE 03

No promo. No bots. No drama theater.

This isn't a marketplace. Self-promo, ragebait, and pile-ons get zero airtime. The drama belongs on screen, not in the comments.
